Our specialisations

CRM & ERP software made for your industry

From manufacturing and CA firms to visa consultants and hospitals, we build software around the way your industry actually works.

  • Manufacturing CRM

    Enquiries, quotations, dealer networks, orders and dispatch tracking in one system.

  • CA Practice CRM

    GST, ITR, TDS and audit compliance tracking, client documents, tasks and billing.

  • Visa & Immigration CRM

    Student, PR, work and tourist visa leads, document checklists and application tracking.

  • Custom ERP Software

    Custom and Odoo ERP connecting sales, purchase, inventory, production, accounts and HR.

  • Real Estate CRM

    Property leads, site visits, unit inventory, bookings and payment milestones.

  • Education & Coaching CRM

    Enquiries, admissions, fee instalments, batches, attendance and parent updates.

  • Hospital & Clinic Software

    Appointments, OPD/IPD, patient records, billing, pharmacy and lab reports.

  • Travel Agency CRM

    Travel enquiries, itinerary builder, quotations, bookings and supplier payments.

  • Insurance Agency CRM

    Leads, multi-insurer policies, renewals, claims and commission tracking.

  • Loan & DSA CRM

    Loan leads, documents, bank logins, sanction and disbursement, EMIs and payouts.

  • HRMS & Payroll

    Attendance, leave, shifts, payroll with PF/ESI/PT/TDS, expenses and appraisals.

  • Inventory & Distribution

    Multi-warehouse stock, barcode, orders, salesman app, delivery and GST billing.
