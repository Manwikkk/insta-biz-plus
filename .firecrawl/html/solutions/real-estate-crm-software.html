Overview

Real Estate CRM Software that fits your business

Real estate sales run on speed. A lead from 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com or a Facebook campaign cools within hours if nobody calls. Once a buyer is interested, your team needs to schedule site visits, share floor plans and pricing, hold units, and then manage agreements and payment schedules for months.

Our real estate CRM connects this entire journey. Leads from portals and ads flow in automatically and are routed to the right sales executive. Site visits are scheduled and tracked, and sales teams see live unit availability with pricing across towers and projects, so double booking never happens.

After booking, the CRM manages demand letters, construction-linked payment schedules, receipts and customer documentation. Channel partners get their own login to register leads and track commissions. Management sees project-wise sales velocity, collections and marketing ROI at a glance.

FAQs

Real Estate CRM software: your questions answered

What does a real estate CRM do?

A real estate CRM manages property leads, site visits, unit inventory, bookings, payment schedules and channel partners in one system, helping builders and brokers convert more enquiries into sales.

Can it pull leads from 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com?

Yes. We integrate with major property portals and ad platforms so leads are created in the CRM automatically and assigned to sales executives.

Can we manage multiple projects and towers?

Yes. The CRM supports multiple projects, towers, floors and unit types with separate pricing, availability and reports.

Does it handle construction-linked payment plans?

Yes. You can define payment milestones per project, generate demand letters automatically and track receipts and outstanding amounts per unit.

Can brokers and channel partners access the CRM?

Yes. Channel partners get a separate portal to register leads, avoid ownership disputes and track commissions.

Is there a mobile app for the sales team?

Yes. Sales executives can manage leads, log calls, schedule site visits and check inventory from a mobile app.
