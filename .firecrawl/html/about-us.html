Our story

Five years. One mission.

Insta Biz Web started in 2020 with one belief: small businesses deserve big-tech quality. What began as a two-person studio in Ahmedabad has grown into a full-stack digital partner - engineering websites, mobile apps, AI agents, CRM systems and growth marketing for 60+ founders across India, the US, UK and Singapore. We’re still founder-led, still obsessed with the craft, and still treat every project like it’s our own.

Our journey

From 2020 to today

Six years of relentless building, learning, and shipping. Scroll through the milestones that shaped us.

  1. 2020Founded

    The beginning

    Mukund Pasi founds Insta Biz Web in Ahmedabad. First office: a single laptop and a lot of caffeine.

  2. 2021

    First 10 clients

    Shipped 10 production websites and 2 mobile apps. Brought on the first co-founder. Set the bar high.

  3. 2022Team scaled

    Founding team formed

    Six co-founders join the journey. Capabilities grow to cover web, mobile, CRM, AI and marketing - under one roof.

  4. 2023

    Going global

    Started serving founders in the US, UK and Singapore. Crossed 30+ shipped projects with a 4.9★ average rating.

  5. 2024AI launched

    AI-first transformation

    Launched Blutec AI products: Ping, Connect, Echo, Scout. Became one of the first Odoo + AI partners in Gujarat.

  6. 2025

    60+ clients & counting

    Crossed 60 active clients globally. 98% positive rating. Two operating branches. Expanding the founding team.

  7. 2026Now

    What&rsquo;s next

    Doubling down on AI agents, vertical SaaS for SMBs, and our first international office. The best is yet to come.
