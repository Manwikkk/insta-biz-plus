# Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: July 16, 2025

[Home](https://www.instabizweb.com/)/Privacy Policy

01

## Introduction

Insta Biz Web (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit [https://www.instabizweb.com](https://www.instabizweb.com/) or use our digital services. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our website.

02

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

- Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, business address
- Business Information: Company name, industry, project requirements
- Payment Information: Billing address, payment method details (processed securely via authorised gateways)
- Communication Records: Messages, emails, and call logs related to our services

### Technical Information

- Website Usage: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited
- Cookies: Preferences, session data, analytics information
- Performance Data: Website speed, user interactions, error logs

03

## How We Use Your Information

1

Service Delivery

To provide, maintain, and improve our digital solutions and services

2

Communication

To respond to enquiries, provide support, and send project updates

3

Payment Processing

To process payments and maintain billing records securely

4

Website Improvement

To analyse usage patterns and improve overall user experience

5

Legal Compliance

To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and court orders

6

Marketing (opt-in)

To send relevant updates if you have opted in to communications

04

## Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

- Service Providers: Trusted partners who assist in service delivery (hosting, payment processing, analytics) under data protection agreements
- Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulations
- Business Protection: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
- Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale, or acquisition of our business

05

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

✓ SSL / TLS encryption for all data transmissions

✓ Secure payment processing through authorised gateways only

✓ Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

✓ Role-based access controls and authentication protocols

✓ Automated data backup and disaster recovery procedures

06

## Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences at any time.

Essential Cookies

Required for website functionality, authentication, and security. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand website usage patterns to improve performance (e.g. Google Analytics).

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings and personalisation choices across sessions.

Marketing Cookies

Used only with your consent to deliver relevant content and measure campaign effectiveness.

07

## Your Privacy Rights

Access

Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information

Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)

Portability

Request transfer of your data to another service provider

Objection

Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes at any time

Restriction

Request restriction of processing while a complaint is being resolved

08

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

Project Data

Duration of project + 3 years for support

Payment Records

7 years (tax & accounting regulations)

Communication Records

2 years for customer service

Website Analytics

2 years (anonymised data only)

09

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than India where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards — including standard contractual clauses — are in place to protect your personal information in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) and international standards such as GDPR where applicable.
