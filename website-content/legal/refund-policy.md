# Refund Policy

Our refund process, eligibility criteria, and timelines — clearly explained.

[Home](https://www.instabizweb.com/)/Refund Policy

01

## Overview

At Insta Biz Web, we are committed to delivering high-quality digital solutions. This refund policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be requested and processed for our services. All refund requests are subject to review and approval — this policy does not guarantee refunds but describes the circumstances under which they may be considered.

02

## Eligibility by Service Type

### Custom Development Services

- Website / App Development: Refund available within 5 days if project requirements are provably not met as agreed in writing
- Business Automation / CRM: 30-day satisfaction guarantee after implementation and handover
- Project initiation advance (30–50%): Non-refundable once discovery / planning work has commenced

### Digital Marketing Services

- Campaign Setup: Full refund if campaign is not launched within the agreed timeframe
- Monthly Retainer Packages: Pro-rated refunds available with 15-day written notice
- Initial Consulting / Strategy Session: Refund available within 48 hours if unsatisfied

03

## Non-Refundable Items

Completed and delivered projects that meet agreed specifications

Third-party licences and subscriptions purchased on behalf of clients

Services where work has been completed and accepted by the client in writing

Custom development work that has been deployed to a production environment

Training and consultation services after the session has been completed

Domain registration, hosting, and third-party API fees

04

## Refund Timelines

Request Review

2–3 business days after submission

Approval Decision

Within 5 business days of review

Processing

5–7 business days after approval

Bank Credit

7–14 days (depends on your bank)

05

## How to Request a Refund

1

Contact Us

Email [[email protected]](mailto:info@instabizweb.com) with subject "Refund Request - [Project Name / Invoice Number]"

2

Provide Details

Include project details, invoice number, payment reference, and reason for the refund request

3

Review

Our team will review your request and respond within 2–3 business days with a decision

4

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are credited to your original payment method within the stated timelines above

06

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

- Project scope changes resulting in a reduction of agreed deliverables
- Early termination of ongoing service retainer contracts
- Unused portions of prepaid service packages (calculated pro-rata)
- By mutual written agreement between Insta Biz Web and the client

07

## Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may request an escalated review by our management team by emailing [info@instabizweb.com](mailto:info@instabizweb.com) with “Refund Dispute” in the subject line. We are committed to resolving all disputes fairly and will respond within 3 business days.

08

## Contact for Refund Requests

Email: [info@instabizweb.com](mailto:info@instabizweb.com)

Subject Format: Refund Request - [Project Name / Invoice #]

Response Time: Within 24–48 business hours

### Important Notice

All refund requests are subject to individual review and management approval. This policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be considered — it does not constitute an automatic entitlement. For specific questions about your project or refund eligibility, please contact our team directly before raising a formal request.
