**Should I use Odoo Community or Enterprise edition?**

Odoo Community is free and covers around 80% of SMB needs. Enterprise adds Studio, official support and some advanced modules. We give honest advice based on your specific requirements - most of our SMB clients start on Community and upgrade only when they hit a real need.

**How long does an Odoo implementation take?**

A clean SMB implementation takes around 6 weeks using our standard playbook. Add 2-4 weeks for heavy custom modules or significant data cleanup. We share a precise, phase-by-phase timeline before kickoff.

**Can you migrate our data from Tally to Odoo?**

Yes. We typically import opening balances and master data as of go-live date, run reconciliation, and keep your old system read-only for historical reference. Full Tally history migration is possible but rarely worth the extra cost.

**Do you provide GST e-invoicing and Indian compliance in Odoo?**

Yes. We configure Odoo's Indian localisation (l10n_in) modules for GST, e-invoicing and e-way bills, and integrate Indian payment gateways - so your Odoo is compliant from day one.

**Do you provide Odoo training and ongoing support?**

Yes. Every implementation includes team training and written SOPs. We also offer ongoing support and AMC retainers covering maintenance, updates and new requirements.

**Do you provide Odoo services near me in Ahmedabad?**

Yes. Our office is in Naranpura, Ahmedabad, and we serve clients across all Ahmedabad neighbourhoods plus Gandhinagar, Surat, Vadodara and Rajkot. On-site and remote Odoo consulting both available.
