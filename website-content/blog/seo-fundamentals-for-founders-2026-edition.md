For a deeper take on the 2026 era, read our [AEO & GEO playbook](https://www.instabizweb.com/blogs/aeo-geo-how-to-rank-in-google-ai-overviews-and-chatgpt).

## How AI search changed everything

Google’s AI Overviews now answer ~30% of search queries directly. ChatGPT and Perplexity sit on top of the funnel for a growing share of B2B research. The implication: you don’t just need to rank - you need to be quotable. AI summarisers cite specific, structured, well-linked sources.

## What still works (and always will)

- Page speed. Lighthouse 95+ is non-negotiable in 2026.
- Schema markup. [Article](https://schema.org/Article), [Product](https://schema.org/Product), [FAQ](https://schema.org/FAQPage), BreadcrumbList - they’re table stakes.
- Topical depth. One deep page beats ten shallow ones. Always.
- Internal links with intent. Help readers and crawlers find related content.
- Real backlinks from real sites. Earned, never bought.

## Founder-friendly checklist

1. Set up [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) + [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) (yes, both)
2. Create one cornerstone article per service
3. Add Article + FAQ schema to every blog post
4. Internal-link new posts to 2 existing ones
5. Audit [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/explore/learn-core-web-vitals) every quarter
6. Track branded vs. non-branded queries separately

SEO compounds. The work you do today shows up in 3-6 months. Start now, stay consistent, and the channel pays you forever.
