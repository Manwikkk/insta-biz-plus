An Odoo implementation in India costs ₹1,50,000 to ₹15,00,000 in Year 1 for most SMBs. The real number depends on three things: your team size, how operationally complex you are, and whether you pick Community or Enterprise. Here’s the data from 18 real projects we’ve scoped or shipped in 2024-2026.

## One-glance summary

| Project type | Year-1 total (typical India 2026) |
| --- | --- |
| Solo / 1-3 users, Community, simple CRM-only | ₹1,50,000 - ₹3,00,000 |
| 5-15 users, Community, CRM + sales | ₹3,00,000 - ₹6,00,000 |
| 15-30 users, Enterprise, CRM + accounting + inventory | ₹6,00,000 - ₹12,00,000 |
| 30-100 users, Enterprise, full ERP + custom modules | ₹12,00,000 - ₹35,00,000 |
| Manufacturing, multi-location, full MRP | ₹15,00,000 - ₹50,00,000+ |

## Where the money actually goes

1. Licenses (Enterprise only): ~₹24,000 per user per year + per-module charges
2. Hosting: Odoo.sh ₹5,000 - ₹25,000/month, or self-host on AWS/DO for ₹3,000 - ₹15,000/month
3. Discovery + business analysis: 10-15% of total budget
4. Configuration: 30-40% of total budget
5. Custom development: 20-30% (this varies wildly)
6. Data migration: 10-15%
7. Training: 5-10%
8. Year-1 support: 10-15%

## Pricing by team size (real client examples)

- Client A: Real-estate SMB, 8 users, Community, CRM + invoicing → ₹2,80,000 Year-1, ₹85,000 Year-2 maintenance
- Client B: Service agency, 22 users, Enterprise, CRM + projects + timesheets → ₹7,40,000 Year-1
- Client C: Manufacturing SMB, 45 users, Enterprise, CRM + inventory + MRP + 3 custom modules → ₹18,50,000 Year-1
- Client D: E-commerce + retail, 12 users, Enterprise, CRM + POS + e-commerce + inventory → ₹9,20,000 Year-1

## By complexity tier

| Tier | What’s included | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | 1-2 modules, stock config, minimal customisation | ₹1.5L - ₹3.5L |
| Standard | 3-5 modules, ~3 custom fields/reports, basic integrations | ₹4L - ₹9L |
| Heavy | 6+ modules, custom modules, third-party integrations (Tally, WhatsApp API, Razorpay) | ₹10L - ₹25L |
| Enterprise multi-location | 10+ modules, multi-company, multi-currency, MRP, BI dashboards | ₹25L - ₹75L |

## Hidden costs nobody quotes upfront

- Data cleanup before migration. Your existing data is dirty - dedup, normalisation, master-data fixes can add ₹50K-₹2L.
- Integration with Indian payment gateways. Razorpay, PayU, Paytm integrations: ₹25K-₹80K each.
- GST e-invoicing / e-way bill. ₹30K-₹1L depending on your billing volume.
- Tally migration / sync. Most Indian SMBs are on Tally - migrating or syncing adds ₹50K-₹2L.
- Custom reports. Each custom report typically ₹8K-₹25K.
- Training beyond Week 6. Most partners include 2 sessions; refresher training later costs ₹15K-₹40K.

## How to save 30-40% legitimately

1. Start with Community. Cover 80% of needs for free. Upgrade to Enterprise only when you hit a specific paywall.
2. Self-host on a single VPS. ₹4,000/month vs ₹15,000+ on Odoo.sh. You give up some convenience.
3. Configure now, customise later (or never). Most teams over-customise. Stock Odoo covers 80% of real workflows.
4. Phase the rollout. Don’t buy all modules in Month 1. Add HR or accounting after the CRM stabilises.
5. Pick a senior partner, not the cheapest. Cheap partners over-customise to bill more hours. A senior team will tell you what NOT to build.
