Zoho CRM is excellent for most Indian SMBs - but not all. Common reasons teams switch: needing ERP+CRM in one stack, hitting Deluge scripting limits, wanting open-source data control, or just needing a more focused sales pipeline tool. Here are the 7 best alternatives we’ve implemented for real Indian clients.

## Why Indian teams leave Zoho in 2026

- Need accounting/inventory inside CRM - Zoho Books and Zoho Inventory are separate products (separate subscriptions)
- Hit Deluge scripting limits - complex automations exceed what Deluge handles cleanly
- Want self-host / open source - Zoho is cloud-only
- Pricing creep - Zoho One bundle is excellent but per-user fees add up past 25-30 users
- Manufacturing scope - Zoho has no MRP/manufacturing module

| # | Alternative | Why pick it over Zoho |

| 1 | Odoo | One stack for CRM + accounting + inventory + HR + manufacturing |

| 3 | HubSpot CRM | Marketing-led B2B with free tier |

## In-depth on each alternative

### 1. Odoo - the best CRM+ERP replacement

If you’re paying for Zoho CRM + Zoho Books + Zoho Inventory + Zoho People, you’re paying 4 separate per-user fees. Odoo gives you all four under one license. For 20+ user SMBs, Odoo is typically 40-60% cheaper than the full Zoho stack. Read [Odoo vs Zoho](https://www.instabizweb.com/blogs/odoo-vs-zoho-crm-2026-comparison), [Odoo implementation cost](https://www.instabizweb.com/blogs/odoo-implementation-cost-india-2026).

### 2. Freshsales - the Indian sibling

Built in Chennai by Freshworks. Comparable per-user pricing to Zoho. AI features (Freddy) are competitive. Often a smoother UX for sales reps. Weaker ecosystem outside Freshworks suite.

### 3. HubSpot CRM - for marketing-led teams

Generous free tier. Best-in-class for content marketing + sales alignment. Premium tier pricing in USD becomes painful in India at scale. Choose if you’re B2B SaaS or content-led.

### 4. Pipedrive - for outbound sales focus

Sales reps love the visual pipeline. Weaker on marketing automation, support workflows, and complex customisation. Per-user pricing in USD. Best for 5-25 outbound rep teams.

### 5. Salesforce Essentials - for future-proofing

Entry-level Salesforce starts at $25/user/month. If you expect to scale to 100+ users in 3 years, starting on Salesforce avoids a future migration. Otherwise overkill.

### 6. Bitrix24 - the free unlimited-users option

Free tier for unlimited users with CRM, tasks, chat, drive, and basic automation. Premium tiers reasonable. UI feels dated. Best for budget-constrained teams that need collaboration + CRM.

### 7. SuiteCRM - the open-source pick

Free, self-hosted, open source. Smaller community than Odoo. No native accounting/inventory. Best for dev-heavy teams that want absolute control with zero licensing cost.

## Quick decision framework

1. Need CRM + accounting + inventory in one stack? → Odoo
2. Need a polished Indian-built Zoho-alike? → Freshsales
3. Marketing-led B2B with content engine? → HubSpot
4. Just need a great pipeline tool? → Pipedrive
5. Need free for unlimited users? → Bitrix24
6. Have devs and want open source? → SuiteCRM or Odoo Community
