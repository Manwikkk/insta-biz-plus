Average mobile app retention after 30 days is 5.7% ([Statista, 2025](https://www.statista.com/statistics/259329/ios-and-android-app-user-retention-rate/)). That means 94 out of 100 people who download your app forget about it within a month. The good news: it’s rarely the technology that fails. It’s the experience.

## The first 30 seconds

We have hard data from our [Chennai Cabs launch](https://www.instabizweb.com/portfolio#mobile) - users who completed onboarding in under 30 seconds were 4× more likely to complete a booking that week. Speed of first-value beats every other metric on day one.

## The 6 principles we follow

1. One job per screen. If a screen has two CTAs of equal weight, you’ve already lost.
2. Skip the sign-up wall. Let people do something before you ask for an account.
3. Optimistic UI. Fake the success state instantly, reconcile with the server. Native-feeling speed without backend gymnastics.
4. Native gestures, native motion. Custom transitions on Android ≠ iOS. Use platform conventions.
5. Notifications you’d send your own friend. Personal, useful, never spammy.
6. Offline-first when it matters. Especially for India - connectivity is a luxury, not a guarantee.

## What we measure

D1, D7, D30 retention. Time-to-first-action. Crash-free sessions percentage. Anything < 99% is a bug, not a feature. Want to see how this translates into real product? [Our mobile services](https://www.instabizweb.com/services#mobile) dig into the full process.
