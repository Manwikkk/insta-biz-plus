Our story

## Five years. One mission.

Insta Biz Web started in 2020 with one belief: small businesses deserve big-tech quality. What began as a two-person studio in Ahmedabad has grown into a full-stack digital partner - engineering websites, mobile apps, AI agents, CRM systems and growth marketing for 60+ founders across India, the US, UK and Singapore. We’re still founder-led, still obsessed with the craft, and still treat every project like it’s our own.

[See what we've built](https://www.instabizweb.com/portfolio)

What drives us

## Mission, vision & the principles we live by

Mission

### Make great tech accessible to every founder.

We believe small businesses deserve the same engineering rigor and design polish as well-funded startups. Our mission is to make that bar reachable for everyone.

Vision

### Be the partner founders trust for the long haul.

Not just an agency for one project - but the team founders call when they’re launching, scaling, pivoting, and re-imagining what’s next.

Values

### Honest. Owner-minded. Quality first.

We tell the truth, even when it’s inconvenient. We treat every project like our own. And we never ship something we wouldn’t use ourselves.

Craft over shortcuts Outcomes over outputs Clarity over cleverness People over process

Our journey

## From 2020 to today

Six years of relentless building, learning, and shipping. Scroll through the milestones that shaped us.

1. 2020 Founded ### The beginning Mukund Pasi founds Insta Biz Web in Ahmedabad. First office: a single laptop and a lot of caffeine.
2. 2021 ### First 10 clients Shipped 10 production websites and 2 mobile apps. Brought on the first co-founder. Set the bar high.
3. 2022 Team scaled ### Founding team formed Six co-founders join the journey. Capabilities grow to cover web, mobile, CRM, AI and marketing - under one roof.
4. 2023 ### Going global Started serving founders in the US, UK and Singapore. Crossed 30+ shipped projects with a 4.9★ average rating.
5. 2024 AI launched ### AI-first transformation Launched Blutec AI products: Ping, Connect, Echo, Scout. Became one of the first Odoo + AI partners in Gujarat.
6. 2025 ### 60+ clients & counting Crossed 60 active clients globally. 98% positive rating. Two operating branches. Expanding the founding team.
7. 2026 Now ### What’s next Doubling down on AI agents, vertical SaaS for SMBs, and our first international office. The best is yet to come.
