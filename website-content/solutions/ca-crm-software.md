Overview

## CA CRM & Practice Management Software that fits your business

A growing CA firm handles hundreds of clients and thousands of recurring compliance tasks every year. GST returns, TDS filings, income tax returns, ROC filings and audits all have hard deadlines, and one missed date means penalties and an unhappy client. Tracking all of it in Excel and WhatsApp simply does not scale.

Our CA CRM and practice management software is built around the Indian compliance calendar. Recurring tasks are generated automatically for each client based on the services they use. Articles and staff see exactly what is due, what is pending from the client and what is ready for partner review.

Clients get a secure portal to upload documents and see filing status, which cuts down on follow-up calls. Partners get a clear view of workload, turnaround time, pending fees and profitability per client, so the firm can grow without chaos.

FAQs

## CA Practice CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is CA practice management software?**

CA practice management software helps chartered accountant firms manage clients, recurring compliance tasks, documents, staff workload and fee billing in one system instead of Excel sheets and WhatsApp.

**### Can it automatically create GST, TDS and ITR tasks for each client?**

Yes. You map services to each client once, and the system generates recurring tasks with due dates every month, quarter or year based on the Indian compliance calendar.

**### Is client data and credential storage secure?**

Yes. We use encrypted storage, role-based access, activity logs and secure hosting. Staff only see the clients and data they are permitted to access.

**### Can clients upload documents themselves?**

Yes. Clients get a portal or mobile-friendly link to upload documents and check the status of their filings, which reduces calls to your office.

**### Does the software file returns on government portals?**

The CRM manages the workflow, documents and status around filings. Actual filing is done on government portals or your existing tax software, and the status is updated in the CRM.

**### Can the software be customised for our firm's processes?**

Yes. Because we build it for you, checklists, approval steps, service types and reports follow your firm's way of working.
