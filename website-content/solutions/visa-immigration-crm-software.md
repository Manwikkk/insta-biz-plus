Overview

## Visa & Immigration CRM Software that fits your business

Visa and immigration consultancies live on volume and trust. Leads pour in from Facebook ads, Google, seminars and walk-ins, and each applicant then moves through counselling, documentation, IELTS or PTE, university or employer applications, fee payments and embassy submission. Managing that in registers and spreadsheets leads to lost leads and anxious clients.

Our visa and immigration CRM is designed around this journey. Every enquiry is captured with source tracking and assigned to a counsellor. Country and visa-type specific checklists tell your team exactly what documents are pending, and each file moves through clear stages so managers can see bottlenecks instantly.

Applicants receive automated WhatsApp and email updates at every stage, and can upload documents through their own portal. Branch-wise and counsellor-wise reports show conversions, revenue and agent commissions, so you can scale across branches with confidence.

FAQs

## Visa & Immigration CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is a visa CRM?**

A visa CRM is software that helps visa and immigration consultants manage leads, applicant documents, application stages, payments and communication in one place, from the first enquiry to the final visa decision.

**### Can the CRM capture leads from Facebook and Google ads?**

Yes. We integrate Meta Lead Ads, Google Ads lead forms and your website so every enquiry lands in the CRM instantly and is assigned to a counsellor.

**### Does it support different checklists for each country?**

Yes. You can configure document checklists and stages per country and visa type, such as Canada study permit, UK student visa, Australia PR or USA tourist visa.

**### Can applicants track their own application status?**

Yes. Applicants get a secure portal and automatic WhatsApp or email updates whenever their file moves to a new stage.

**### Can it manage multiple branches and sub-agents?**

Yes. The CRM supports multiple branches, role-based access and sub-agent tracking with commission reports.

**### Is applicant data kept secure?**

Yes. Passport and personal documents are stored with encryption, access controls and audit logs on secure cloud hosting.
