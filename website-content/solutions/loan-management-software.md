Overview

## Loan CRM & Loan Management Software that fits your business

Loan distribution is a document-heavy, multi-party process. A single case moves between the customer, the DSA, connectors and multiple banks or NBFCs, each with its own checklist and status. Tracking this on WhatsApp and Excel means lost files, slow turnaround and payout disputes.

Our loan CRM gives every case a clear trail from lead to disbursement. Product-specific document checklists, eligibility notes and bank-wise login tracking make sure files are complete and nothing waits unnoticed. Connectors can submit leads through their own portal and track status transparently.

For NBFCs and lenders, we extend the system into loan management with EMI schedules, collections, overdue tracking and reports. Payout calculations for DSAs and connectors are automated, and management dashboards show volumes, conversion and revenue by product, bank and team.

FAQs

## Loan & DSA CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is loan CRM software?**

Loan CRM software helps DSAs, loan agents and lenders manage loan leads, documents, bank logins, sanction and disbursement tracking, and payouts in one system.

**### Can it track a case across multiple banks?**

Yes. Each case can be logged with multiple banks or NBFCs, with separate status, queries and outcomes tracked for each.

**### Can connectors submit leads?**

Yes. Connectors and sub-DSAs get their own portal or app to submit leads, upload documents and view status and payouts.

**### Does it include EMI and collections management?**

Yes. For NBFCs and lenders, we include EMI schedules, repayments, overdue tracking and collection follow-ups.

**### Is customer financial data secure?**

Yes. We use encryption, role-based access, audit logs and secure hosting to protect KYC and financial documents.

**### Can payouts be calculated automatically?**

Yes. Payout rules by lender, product and volume slab are configured once and calculated automatically for every disbursed case.
