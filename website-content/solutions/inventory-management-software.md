Overview

## Inventory & Distribution Management Software that fits your business

For traders, wholesalers and distributors, inventory is cash. Too much stock blocks working capital, too little loses sales, and stock that does not match the books leads to losses nobody can explain. Most of these problems start with manual stock registers and disconnected billing software.

Our inventory and distribution software tracks stock in real time across warehouses, godowns and vans, with barcode and batch or serial tracking where you need it. Reorder levels and demand trends tell you what to buy and when, and every inward and outward movement is recorded with a clear audit trail.

On the sales side, salesmen book orders on a mobile app with live stock and scheme details, deliveries are planned and tracked, and GST invoices with e-way bills are generated automatically. Outstanding payments by retailer are visible to salesmen in the field, helping you collect faster.

FAQs

## Inventory & Distribution software: your questions answered

**### What is inventory management software?**

Inventory management software tracks stock levels, movements and valuation across locations in real time, helping businesses avoid stock-outs, reduce excess inventory and prevent losses.

**### Does it support multiple warehouses?**

Yes. You can manage stock across any number of warehouses, godowns, shops and delivery vans with inter-location transfers.

**### Can salesmen book orders from a mobile app?**

Yes. Salesmen get an app to book orders with live stock, schemes and retailer outstanding, even in low-network areas.

**### Does it generate GST invoices and e-way bills?**

Yes. The system creates GST-compliant invoices, e-invoices and e-way bills directly from sales orders.

**### Can it integrate with Tally?**

Yes. Sales, purchases and receipts can sync with Tally so your accountant continues working in familiar software.

**### Does it support barcode and batch tracking?**

Yes. We support barcode printing and scanning, batch and expiry tracking and serial numbers based on your products.
