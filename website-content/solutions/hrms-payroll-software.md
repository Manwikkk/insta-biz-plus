Overview

## HRMS & Payroll Software that fits your business

HR teams in growing companies spend the last week of every month compiling attendance from biometric devices, reconciling leave, calculating overtime and building payroll in Excel. One wrong formula leads to incorrect salaries, compliance issues and unhappy employees.

Our HRMS and payroll software automates this cycle. Attendance flows in from biometric devices or the mobile app with GPS, leave and shifts are applied automatically, and payroll is processed with PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS calculated correctly. Payslips are generated and shared in a click.

Employees get a self-service app to mark attendance, apply for leave, claim expenses and download payslips, which cuts HR queries dramatically. Management gets reports on headcount, attrition, attendance trends and salary costs across departments and locations.

FAQs

## HRMS & Payroll software: your questions answered

**### What is HRMS software?**

HRMS (Human Resource Management System) software manages employee data, attendance, leave, payroll, compliance and performance in one system, reducing manual HR work.

**### Does the payroll handle PF, ESI, PT and TDS?**

Yes. Payroll includes Indian statutory deductions such as PF, ESI, professional tax, LWF and TDS, with the reports and challans you need for filing.

**### Can it integrate with our biometric machines?**

Yes. We integrate with commonly used biometric and face recognition devices so attendance flows into the HRMS automatically.

**### How do field employees mark attendance?**

Field staff can mark attendance from the mobile app with GPS location and optional geo-fencing and selfie verification.

**### Can employees download payslips themselves?**

Yes. The employee self-service app lets staff download payslips, check leave balances, apply for leave and submit expense claims.

**### Can the HRMS integrate with our accounting software?**

Yes. Payroll journal entries can be exported or synced to Tally or your ERP.
