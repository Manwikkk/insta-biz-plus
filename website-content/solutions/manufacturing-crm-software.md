## Challenges our Manufacturing CRM solves

If these sound familiar, your business has outgrown spreadsheets and generic tools.

### Enquiries lost across channels

Leads from IndiaMART, website, calls and exhibitions sit in different inboxes and many are never followed up.

Overview

## Manufacturing CRM Software that fits your business

Most manufacturers still run sales on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and the memory of a few senior salespeople. Enquiries from IndiaMART, TradeIndia, exhibitions and dealers arrive through different channels, quotations are revised five times over email, and nobody knows which order is stuck in production or waiting for dispatch.

Insta Biz Web builds manufacturing CRM software that fits your actual sales cycle, whether you sell machines, components, chemicals, packaging, textiles or engineering products. We capture enquiries automatically, generate branded quotations with your price lists and taxes, track follow-ups for every salesperson and dealer, and connect confirmed orders with production and dispatch status.

Because the CRM is built for you, it speaks your language: product variants, MOQ, custom specifications, dealer schemes, credit limits and GST invoicing. It can integrate with Tally, your existing ERP, WhatsApp Business and IndiaMART lead APIs, so your team works in one place instead of five.

FAQs

## Manufacturing CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is manufacturing CRM software?**

Manufacturing CRM software manages the complete sales cycle of a manufacturer: capturing enquiries, sending quotations, following up, managing dealers and distributors, converting orders and tracking dispatch and after-sales service in one system.

**### Can the CRM import leads from IndiaMART and TradeIndia automatically?**

Yes. We integrate with the IndiaMART and TradeIndia lead APIs so new enquiries are created in the CRM automatically, assigned to the right salesperson and tracked until closure.

**### Does it integrate with Tally or our existing ERP?**

Yes. We regularly integrate CRMs with Tally Prime and ERP systems such as Odoo so customers, orders and invoices stay in sync without double entry.

**### Can our dealers and distributors use the CRM?**

Yes. We can include a dealer portal and mobile app where dealers place orders, check schemes, view outstanding payments and track dispatch.

**### How long does it take to build a manufacturing CRM?**

A focused first version is usually ready in 4 to 8 weeks depending on modules and integrations. We release in phases so your team can start using core features early.

**### Is the CRM available on mobile for field sales?**

Yes. Field sales teams get a mobile app with visit check-in, order booking, expense claims and offline support for low-network areas.
