Overview

## Insurance CRM Software that fits your business

Insurance agencies grow on renewals and referrals. But when policies from multiple insurers are tracked in spreadsheets, renewal dates are missed, customers switch to another agent, and commission reconciliation becomes a monthly headache.

Our insurance CRM keeps every customer, family member and policy in one place, across life, health, motor and general insurance. Renewal reminders go out automatically to customers and your team, and lapsed or upcoming policies are highlighted on the dashboard.

The CRM also tracks new leads, quotes, claims assistance and commission payouts by insurer and product. For agencies with sub-agents or POSPs, it provides hierarchy-wise performance and payout reports, making your insurance business easier to scale.

FAQs

## Insurance Agency CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is insurance CRM software?**

Insurance CRM software helps agents and brokers manage leads, customers, policies from multiple insurers, renewals, claims and commissions in one system.

**### Can it send automatic renewal reminders?**

Yes. The CRM sends renewal reminders by WhatsApp, SMS and email before due dates, and alerts your team about upcoming and lapsed policies.

**### Does it support policies from multiple insurance companies?**

Yes. You can manage life, health, motor and general policies from any insurer in one place.

**### Can we track commissions from insurers?**

Yes. The commission module tracks expected and received commissions and helps reconcile insurer statements.

**### Can we import our existing policy data?**

Yes. We import customers and policies from Excel or your current software during setup.

**### Does it support sub-agents and POSPs?**

Yes. You can manage multi-level agent hierarchies with role-based access and payout reports.
