Overview

## Hospital & Clinic Management Software that fits your business

Clinics and hospitals juggle appointments, walk-ins, consultations, lab tests, pharmacy and billing, often on disconnected software or paper files. Patients wait longer, records get lost, and revenue leaks through unbilled services and pharmacy stock that is not tracked properly.

Our hospital and clinic management software brings every department onto one platform. Patients book appointments online or by WhatsApp, reception registers them in seconds, and doctors see complete history, prescriptions and reports on screen. Lab and pharmacy are connected, so orders and results flow automatically.

Billing covers OPD, IPD, packages, TPA and insurance claims, with detailed reports on revenue by department and doctor. Automated follow-up reminders and digital reports improve patient experience and repeat visits. The system is built with role-based access and secure hosting to protect sensitive health data.

## Hospital & Clinic Software software: your questions answered

**### What is hospital management software?**

Hospital management software (HMS) digitises hospital and clinic operations including appointments, patient records, OPD, IPD, billing, pharmacy and lab, so staff work faster and patients get better care.

**### Is the software suitable for small clinics?**

Yes. We build lighter versions for single-doctor and small clinics with appointments, EMR, prescriptions and billing, and scale up as you grow.

**### Can patients book appointments online?**

Yes. Patients can book through your website, a patient app or WhatsApp, and receive confirmations and reminders automatically.

**### Does it support insurance and TPA billing?**

Yes. The billing module supports TPA and insurance workflows, pre-authorisation tracking and claim documentation.

**### How is patient data protected?**

We use encrypted storage, role-based access, audit logs, regular backups and secure hosting to protect health records.

**### Can it integrate with lab machines?**

Yes. We can integrate supported lab analysers so results are recorded directly into patient reports without manual entry.
