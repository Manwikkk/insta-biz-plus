Overview

## Custom ERP Software Development that fits your business

As a business grows, every department picks its own tool. Sales uses Excel, accounts uses Tally, the warehouse keeps a register and production plans on a whiteboard. Data is entered three times, reports take days, and nobody trusts the numbers. An ERP fixes this by making every department work on one shared database.

Insta Biz Web offers two routes. For businesses with standard processes, we implement and customise Odoo ERP, a proven open-source platform with modules for almost every function. For businesses with unique workflows, we build a fully custom ERP from scratch so the software follows your process, not the other way around.

Either way, you get GST-ready invoicing, multi-location inventory, purchase and production planning, role-based access and dashboards that show profitability, stock and cash flow in real time. We handle data migration, training and ongoing support, so your ERP keeps pace as your business grows.

FAQs

## Custom ERP Software software: your questions answered

**### What is ERP software?**

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) software connects all core business functions such as sales, purchase, inventory, production, accounting and HR into one system with a shared database, so information flows automatically between departments.

**### Should we choose Odoo or a fully custom ERP?**

Odoo is ideal when your processes are close to standard and you want a faster, cost-effective rollout. A fully custom ERP is better when you have unique workflows that off-the-shelf software cannot handle. We recommend the right route after a free discovery session.

**### How long does ERP implementation take?**

A typical SME implementation takes 2 to 4 months, depending on modules, data migration and customisation. We go live in phases so critical modules start delivering value early.

**### Is the ERP GST compliant?**

Yes. We configure GST invoicing, HSN/SAC codes, e-invoice and e-way bill generation and GST return reports as per Indian regulations.

**### Can we migrate our data from Tally and Excel?**

Yes. We migrate masters and opening balances such as customers, vendors, products, stock and ledgers from Tally, Excel or your existing software.

**### Is the ERP cloud-based or on-premise?**

Both options are available. Most clients choose secure cloud hosting for anywhere access, but we also deploy on your own servers when required.
