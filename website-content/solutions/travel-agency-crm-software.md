Overview

## Travel Agency CRM Software that fits your business

Travel agencies compete on speed and personalisation. When a family asks for a Kerala or Dubai package, the agency that sends a clear, attractive itinerary first usually wins. Yet most agents still build quotes by copying old Word files and calculating costs manually across hotels, transport and activities.

Our travel agency CRM captures enquiries from ads, website, WhatsApp and walk-ins, then lets your team build day-wise itineraries with hotels, transfers and sightseeing from a reusable library. Costs and margins are calculated automatically, and a branded PDF or web quote is sent to the traveller in minutes.

Once confirmed, the CRM tracks bookings, vouchers, traveller documents, customer payments and supplier payables. Automated reminders go out for pending payments and travel dates, and management sees destination-wise sales, margins and team performance.

## Travel Agency CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is a travel agency CRM?**

A travel agency CRM helps travel agents and tour operators manage enquiries, build itineraries and quotations, track bookings and payments, and communicate with travellers from one system.

**### Can we create itineraries and quotes quickly?**

Yes. The itinerary builder uses a reusable library of hotels, transfers and activities, and calculates costs and margins automatically so you can send a branded quote in minutes.

**### Does it handle GST and TCS on tour packages?**

Yes. Invoicing can be configured for applicable GST and TCS rules on domestic and international packages.

**### Can we track payments to hotels and suppliers?**

Yes. Supplier payables are tracked per booking so you can reconcile what you owe with what customers have paid.

**### Can the CRM send WhatsApp updates to travellers?**

Yes. We integrate WhatsApp Business API for quotes, payment reminders, pre-trip checklists and post-trip feedback.

**### Is it suitable for small travel agencies?**

Yes. We start with the modules you need and add more as your agency grows.
