Overview

## Education CRM & Institute Management Software that fits your business

Coaching institutes, schools and colleges receive hundreds of enquiries every admission season from ads, walk-ins, seminars and referrals. Without a system, counsellors lose track of follow-ups, demo classes are not scheduled, and interested students join a competitor.

Our education CRM captures every enquiry, schedules counselling and demo sessions, and nudges counsellors to follow up at the right time. Once a student enrols, the same system manages admission forms, fee structures and instalments, batch allocation, attendance and test results.

Parents and students stay informed through automated WhatsApp and SMS updates about fees, attendance and results. Management gets clear reports on enquiry sources, counsellor conversions, fee collections and batch occupancy, so every admission season is better than the last.

## Education & Coaching CRM software: your questions answered

**### What is an education CRM?**

An education CRM helps schools, colleges and coaching institutes manage student enquiries, counselling, admissions and communication, so more enquiries convert into enrolled students.

**### Can it manage fee instalments and reminders?**

Yes. You can set fee structures with instalments and discounts, accept online payments and send automatic reminders to students and parents before and after due dates.

**### Is there an app for parents and students?**

Yes. Parents and students can view attendance, results, notices, homework and fee status on a mobile app or web portal.

**### Can it handle multiple branches or centres?**

Yes. The system supports multiple branches with centralised reporting and branch-level access control.

**### Does it integrate with biometric attendance devices?**

Yes. We can integrate supported biometric and RFID devices so attendance is recorded automatically.

**### Can we run online classes through it?**

We can integrate Zoom or Google Meet for scheduling and joining live classes, and add a basic learning module for notes and recorded content if needed.
